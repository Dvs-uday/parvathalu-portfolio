# Build a Premium Interactive Portfolio Website — Damarla Parvathalu

Build a complete, modern, premium, interactive, and fully responsive personal portfolio website for **Damarla Parvathalu**, a Senior Sales Manager at **Star Health and Allied Insurance Co. Ltd., India**.

The website should showcase his professional identity, career experience, leadership, achievements, insurance expertise, team, and his branch.

## IMPORTANT: Build the Complete Visual Experience First

Do not create a wireframe or a website filled with empty placeholder boxes.

**Build the complete website with realistic sample content, beautiful photography, professional visual assets, polished layouts, and fully working interactions from the first version.**

Use realistic placeholder information wherever specific details are unknown, but make it easy to replace later.

Use professional stock images or appropriate generated imagery for:
- Professional executive portraits
- Insurance professionals
- Sales teams
- Office and branch environments
- Customer interactions
- Team meetings
- Awards and recognition
- Corporate environments

The first version should already look like a finished, launch-ready website.
